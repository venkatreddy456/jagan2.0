About Y. S. Jagan Mohan Reddy

Y. S. Jagan Mohan Reddy is an Indian politician from Andhra Pradesh. He served as the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh from 2019 to 2024.

He is the founder and president of the YSR Congress Party (YSRCP). His political career has included work in state politics and public administration in Andhra Pradesh.

The Jagan 2.0 page is designed as a simple informational presentation featuring a photo slider, biography, political career information, and key themes associated with his public work.
